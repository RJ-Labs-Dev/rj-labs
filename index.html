      Why this matters

      People look you up before they walk in. If what they find is wrong, some of them go somewhere else.

      And you would not hear about it. A customer who picks another place does not call to tell you why.

      Your listing is the first thing they see

      When someone searches your name, Google and Apple Maps show a box with your hours, phone number, and directions. That box comes up before your website does.

      Google and Apple are separate

      iPhones come with Apple Maps. Fixing your Google listing does nothing for Apple Maps, and fixing Apple does nothing for Google. Each one has to be checked on its own.

      If nobody is watching it, it can change

      Google lets the public suggest changes to a business listing, like new hours or a different website link. If you do not control your listing, those changes can go live without you knowing.

      How we work

      If someone calls saying your website has a problem, be careful. Here is how we work instead.

      1. Everything stays in your name.

        Your web address, your Google listing, your Apple Maps listing, your accounts, and your card are all in your name. RJ Labs never owns your web address. If you stop working with us, you keep all of it.

      2. We never ask for your passwords.

        Google and Apple both let you give someone access to your listing from your own account. Google calls this adding a manager. You can take that access away whenever you want. That is how we get in. If anyone asks you for a password, say no.

      3. We are local, in Flagstaff. We will meet you in person.

        At your shop, over coffee, wherever is easy. You will know exactly who you are dealing with.

      4. Checking your listings is free. No obligation.

        We will tell you what we find. If everything looks fine, we will say so and that is the end of it.

      What we do not do

      We build websites and fix listings. We are not a computer security company. If you think someone has broken into your website itself, you need a website security specialist, and we will tell you that up front rather than guess. Two well known ones are Sucuri and Wordfence.
