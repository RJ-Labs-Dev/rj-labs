What we automate

Three places most owners lose time.

These are areas we can scope and build for your business. Each project starts with one workflow, set up around how you already work.

Where we usually start

Customer follow up

Leads and customers hear back on time, and fewer conversations slip through the cracks.

  • Email and text follow up workflows
  • Routing new inquiries and setting reminders
  • Outreach to customers who have agreed to hear from you
  • Tracking replies and handing conversations back to you

Daily operations

Less time sorting, copying, and chasing. More of the day spent on the actual work.

  • Inbox organization and summaries
  • Turning incoming requests into tasks
  • Connecting forms, calendars, spreadsheets, and the tools you use
  • Daily briefings and reminders

Content workflows

Turn what you already capture into content without starting from a blank page.

  • Organizing footage and ideas
  • Preparing scripts, captions, and drafts
  • Routing content to you for approval
  • Connecting publishing and reporting steps where the platform supports it

How it works

Start with one workflow. Expand when it proves useful.

No big rollout. We pick a single repetitive task, make it work well, and build from there.

  1. 01

    Find the right task

    We talk through what you do by hand each week and choose one workflow that is repetitive and worth the effort. We agree on its scope and what success looks like.

  2. 02

    Build around your business

    We connect the tools you already use where they allow it. AI is used where it helps, and ordinary automation handles the predictable steps.

  3. 03

    Test and launch

    We test with real scenarios before anything reaches a customer, then launch with the approvals you want in place.

  4. 04

    Monitor and improve

    Ongoing monitoring and maintenance is available. We adjust as your business changes and add the next workflow when it makes sense.

You stay in control

Sensitive messages and important actions can require your approval before anything happens. When something falls outside what the system should handle, it is handed back to a person instead of guessed at.

Questions

Straight answers.

What can you automate?

Repetitive work that follows a pattern: following up with leads and customers, routing inquiries, organizing your inbox, turning requests into tasks, reminders and daily briefings, and preparing content drafts. Whether a specific task is a good fit depends on the tools involved and how predictable it is. Some things are better left to a person, and we will tell you when that is the case.

Do I need to change the software I use?

Usually not. We try to build around the tools you already have. Compatibility depends on what those tools allow other software to connect to. If something cannot be connected cleanly, we will explain the options during scoping before you commit to anything.

Can I approve messages before they are sent?

Yes. Customer messages and important actions can be set up to wait for your approval. Many owners start that way and only loosen it for routine steps once they trust how the system behaves.

What happens if something fails?

Systems are designed so that when a step cannot be completed or a situation is unclear, the item is handed back to a person rather than guessed at. How the system is monitored, and how problems are reported and fixed, is defined in your engagement. No automation is perfect, which is why important steps can require your approval.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is scoped individually, because the work depends on the workflow and the tools involved. You will get a written scope and a quote before any work starts. Some workflows also have running costs, such as software subscriptions or text messaging usage. We explain those during scoping so there are no surprises.

Can you maintain the system afterward?

Yes. Ongoing monitoring and maintenance is available after launch. What is covered and how quickly issues are handled is written into the agreement, so you know exactly what to expect.
