Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated October 16, 2026.

This page explains what happens to information you share with RJ Labs LLC through rj-labs.com.

What the contact form collects

When you send the form, we receive your name, email address, and your description of what you would like to automate. Your business name, website, and phone number are optional.

Where it goes

Form submissions are stored in a Google Forms account owned by RJ Labs, so Google processes them on our behalf under its own terms. Only RJ Labs reads them.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and talk through your request.
  • We do not sell or share your information, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Tracking

This site does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the service.

Your choices

To ask what we have from you, or to have it deleted, email rowan@rj-labs.com.
